Tab Transcribe

Pick a recording or video of guitar and/or bass. The app listens for notes with a neural pitch model (Spotify's Basic Pitch), works out which notes belong to the guitar and which to the bass, and writes the most playable fingering as tablature.

Everything runs on this device; your recordings are never uploaded.

Tips: isolated or clearly mixed guitar/bass works best. Vocals, keys or heavy distortion can add extra notes. Use Options to force the instrument, tuning, capo or tempo, then check the result with Transcription playback.
